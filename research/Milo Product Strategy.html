The Verdict

→ WhatsApp Customer Support Agent for Small Businesses

This is the strongest fit for Milo as it exists today — English-only, 202M params, tool-calling trained.

  1. No Indic languages needed yet — urban small businesses operate in English/Hinglish on WhatsApp
  2. Tool-calling IS the product — exactly what you just trained
  3. 202M is a feature, not a bug — ₹0.50/conversation vs ₹5–10 for GPT-4 competitors
  4. Clear revenue — ₹499–999/month, undercuts Yellow.ai/Gupshup by 10×
  5. RAG covers the knowledge gap — each business's FAQ becomes the knowledge base
  6. Your agency experience gives you domain credibility with small businesses
  7. Expansion path — English → Hindi → more languages → more verticals

MVP Spec

What to build first

  • WhatsApp Business API integration (Twilio or Gupshup as provider)
  • Milo with tool-calling: product catalog lookup, order status, pricing calculator
